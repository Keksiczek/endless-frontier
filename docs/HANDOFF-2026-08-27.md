## 1. Finish `ColonyLogEntry.subject` — small, and it completes a shipped feature

**State:** `15 of 76` `journal.append(…)` calls set `subject: .pawn(id)`.

A colonist's inspector card now has a *"what happened to them"* section
(`GameViewModel.history(of:)`, shown by `PawnInspectorCard`), and it reads
`ColonyLogEntry.subject`. So the feature is only as good as the subjects — and
today, for most colonists, the section is empty.

Unset on: births, coming-of-age, most work and construction lines, arrivals and
departures, faith. Set on: friendships, quarrels, weddings, first case of a
plague, hunter gored or killed, raid deaths.

**Do:** walk `journal.append` call sites in `Core/…/Engine/`, add
`subject: .pawn(id)` wherever the line is about one person. Where a line is
about two (a wedding, a quarrel), the convention already in the code is to hang
it off the first. Do **not** subject lines that are about the colony as a whole.

**Guard it:** a test asserting a minimum share of pawn-facing lines carry a
subject would rot; better is a test that a colonist who is born, grows up,
marries and dies has a non-empty `history(of:)`.

## 2. The crafting gate — biggest effect on play, and the one real open item from the audit

**State:** `245 of 420` recipes carry no `requiresTech`. Recipes are gated
almost entirely by *building*: `workshop` 123, `hut` 76, `hunters_lodge` 56,
none 47. Build one workshop and **123 recipes appear at once**.

Already done and *not* a substitute: the panel folds into groups with counts,
sorts best-first by `QuartermasterEngine.worth`, shows damage/armour on gear
rows, and marks anything that beats what the colony carries. That made 400
recipes navigable. It did not make them arrive at a sane rate.

**Do:** give the ungated recipes a `requiresTech` derived from **material chain
depth** — a recipe's tier is one above the deepest thing it consumes. The
distribution is already measured (`BACKLOG` §18 and the probe work): depth 5–6
recipes are 100% tech-gated already, depth 1 is 73% ungated, so the generation
got lazier the earlier it went. The early tech ladder is sitting unused —
`snares` gates one recipe, `pit_props` and `yokes` gate none.

**The rule that matters here:** never push a recipe later than the era its
*inputs* come from, or you make it unmakeable (rule 6 / rule 95 shape). Verify
by running a colony and checking nothing that was craftable becomes permanently
blocked.

## 4. The iron chain — measure before touching

`WoodProbe` (run with `EF_PROBE=1 swift test --package-path Core --filter
WoodProbe`, ~17 min) reports `charcoal` at **0 in every reading** across two
centuries, with `Smelt Iron (Bloomery)` short of charcoal.

Wood is fixed and abundant now (3223 on the shelf at year 200), so charcoal is
being made and consumed in the same breath. That is a real economy rather than
the dead chain it used to be — **but nobody has checked whether the iron half
keeps up with demand.** Extend the probe with iron ore / ingots / steel columns
before changing any rate. Print the distribution first (rule 23); a rate that
never fires is either a small chance or a chance multiplied by something that is
always the same, and those want opposite fixes (rule 90).

## Things that are done — do not rebuild them

- **Pause.** `GameViewModel.PauseReason`, manual button in `StatusStrip`, auto
  on raid and decision, off-switch in Settings. Two traps already paid for:
  resume carries `lastRealTimestamp` forward (or a pause *causes* a catch-up),
  and the trigger is keyed on the thing (`pausedForID`) rather than edge-detected
  across a tick (most decisions arrive during a catch-up).
- **Offensive war.** `TribeWarEngine` — a march is a `RegionExpedition` and a
  `SiteEncounter`, deliberately not a second combat system.
- **Wood.** Loggers plant when the stand is at its floor (`FloraEngine.tended`).
- **Raider identity.** `Combatant.name`, generated at muster from the siege seed.
- **Death records.** Raid dead are named; maulings are `.death`; the journal has
  a lens.
- **Notifications** for a raid and for either decision queue.
