## 3. The last commit, in detail

Both verbs close `docs/NEIGHBOURS.md` §5.

**The embassy.** `Pawn.envoyToTribeID` is the *single* record of a posting, and
`isAway` includes it — so everything that already respects absence (the labour
engine, the food they do not eat at home) takes the cost with **no second seam
cut for it**. No "posted since" field: standing accrues `envoyStandingPerYear`
every year the post stands, so the accumulated total already is the duration.
The price the label cannot show is that the colonist is *gone*.

**Tribute you pay.** `Tribe.tributePerYear`, collected in `collectTribute`.
Two things it stands on:

- **Half a tribute buys half a peace** — relief scales with what is paid, so the
  amount is a real decision.
- **Stopping costs more than never starting**, and **a colony that cannot pay
  has broken it** — deliberately identical to one that will not, because a
  people on the receiving end cannot tell those apart.

## 4. Open work, with numbers against it

### 4.1 Genes do not move — *diagnosed, not measured, not fixed*

Keks, at the end of the session: *"vlohy by bylo fajn pořešit, nyní se drží
stejné."* He is right, and the arithmetic says why before any probe runs:

- `Genes.blended` is the **midpoint of both parents** — halves the variance
  every generation;
- `Genes.mutated` is **±0.09 uniform** — mean-zero, so it adds spread and no
  direction.

Fixed point of those two is a standing deviation of about **0.073**: people
differ from each other, and the colony's *mean* never leaves 0.5 unless
something pushes it. Selection exists but is weak — `courage` and `industry`
each add ten and eight years of life, `fertility` moves the fertile window.

**Do not widen the mutation.** That makes noise, not drift. The mean only moves
if genes decide *who survives and who has children*, which is the half that is
thin.

This matters twice over: **"gene drift" is one of the chronicle's generated
insights**, so if the mean never moves the insight has nothing to say. Keks's
two asks — "make the chronicle worth something" and "genes stay the same" — are
one problem from two sides.

**First step: measure.** Add gene means and spread over two centuries to
`GrowthProbe` (or a small probe of its own) and read it before touching a
constant.

### 4.2 Grudge saturates at the ceiling for every people

`DiplomacyProbe`, 200 years: every one of five peoples ends at 110. A number
every neighbour shares stops telling a people you have wronged from one you have
not.

**Attack the relief, not the source.** Capping crowding's contribution was tried
and took the measured war count from **67 to 2** (rule 71) — reverted, with a
regression test. Trade and marriage take three off a grudge that grows by eight
a year, and §8.5 claimed they "work it off". They do not.

### 4.3 Courtship has a ceiling

`GrowthProbe`: `slots` (fertile adults with all five relationship slots full)
reads **90–100% from year ten**, and `wed` falls **70 → 8** while the population
climbs 55 → 283. Not hurting the curve yet — births still outrun deaths — but it
is a ceiling that has been there the whole time.

**The old claim that a colony peaks at 69 and decays to 29 is stale.** The
housing work fixed it; nobody re-ran the probe to notice. Measured today:
21 → 283 over two hundred years, monotonic.

### 4.4 The council deepens rather than widens

Six road edges reach rail while `road` and `paved` read zero — not because they
are unreachable (`RoadLadderTests` proves a bare edge gets a road first) but
because the council always builds the best-scoring edge and carries the same few
past those grades. A realm with one superb trunk route is a defensible answer;
so is a web of ordinary roads. **Nobody has decided which this game wants.**

## 7. Rules this session added — 65 to 72

They are in `docs/RULES.md` in full. The short forms, because every one cost
real time today:

- **65** A budget for a bank you have not measured is a guess. `trackThreshold`
  was 60 against a measured total traffic of **four**.
- **66** A ladder that hands back its top rung has only one rung — the council
  laid railways across bare country and never a road.
- **67** "Nothing was recorded" and "nothing happened" are the same number and
  different bugs. Assert the precondition first.
- **68** A metric aimed at the wrong journey argues convincingly for breaking
  something that works. Four rounds chased a 5% figure measuring a wilderness
  hex nothing would ever road toward.
- **69** A rate that only fires on an event is not a rate. Roads are worn by
  **neighbours going back and forth**, not by thirteen caravans in two centuries.
- **70** Editing a source file while its test build runs fails the *build*. A
  twenty-minute probe run died to `input file ... was modified during the build`.
- **71** Fixing a saturated number by capping its source caps everything
  downstream. 67 wars became 2.
- **72** Two changes, one measurement, and you cannot tell which did what.

## 8. Practical notes

  crashed the simulator with `Invalid device state` and left a hung `xcodebuild`
  that read as a broken project. `~/Library/Developer/Xcode/iOS DeviceSupport`

  app tests use the *simulator*.

  over it; give each run its own `--scratch-path` or they queue silently.

  asking for anything else fails in a way that reads like a broken project.
