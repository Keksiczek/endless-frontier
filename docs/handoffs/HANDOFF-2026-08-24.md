### The one finding everything else hung off

**Every extracted material was at zero.** Keks's valley, 113 years in: seven
trees all below workable growth, nine outcrops all broken, and no massif under a
plains map. Only `field` deposits regrew.

31 of 59 buildings need a crafted good; every crafted good starts at `wood`;
`smelt_iron` needs charcoal which needs wood. So the whole crafted half of the
game had been unreachable for most of the colony's life — which is why his town
was 98 buildings of the 28 kinds that need nothing made, with no power and no
factories, and why the iron ore on his shelf stood at exactly 193 across a
decade of replay.

Two traps found in the fixing, both general:

1. **Harvesting under breeding age.** `minimumWorkableGrowth` was 0.35 and
   bearing age is 0.5, so every sapling met an axe before it could be a parent.
   With only a seed stand in place the wood came back to 48 trees over fifty
   years with **zero bearing the whole way** — a healthy count and no future.
2. **A strict `min` tie-break is a policy.** Three deposits at zero are tied;
   the tie went to the lowest array index, every pass, so stone and iron were
   fed for forty years while the clay stood one unit short of a brick.

Measured after, sixty years from his save: 7 → 50 trees with 17–20 bearing and
holding, timber bundles 0 → 17, stone 111 → 336, bricks 7 → 37, and generators
affordable for the first time — the council picks the charcoal kiln itself.

## Open, and honest about it

1. **Wood is now the binding constraint on everything downstream.** `charcoal`
   is still 0 and so `iron_ingot` is still 0, so nothing modern can be made or
   drawn. The chain is *reachable* now rather than dead, but a plains valley of
   fifty trees does not obviously feed a colony of 130. Measure the sustainable
   yield against `saw_timber` + `burn_charcoal` before tuning either.
2. **Herbs have the same shape, unmeasured.** 0.3 of a 246 capacity: foragers
   out-pull `depositRegrowthFraction` about 8×.
3. **`AgentMotion` routes per leg through a lock-guarded cache** (`WalkRoutes`).
   It has not been profiled against the 150 ms layout budget in `TribeCampTests`
   with a full town on screen.
4. **The settlement span still does not follow the grid.** `grownOutward` takes
   in a ring of four whenever a building will not fit, so a growing town's tiles
   shrink; the opening zoom went to 2.8 to cover it. The real fix drags the
   charted ground (`LocalMapGenerator`) and the rock clearance
   (`StoneEngine.colonyClearance`) with it.
5. **Interiors are still keyed off `switch glyph`** with 21 hard-coded `Fitting`
   cases, so a medieval workshop and a far-future assembly plant share crates.
   This is the next thing in the same shape as `ArmourProfile` — move fittings
   into a data bank with an era range and a shape spec, then generate.
6. Everything still open from `docs/HANDOFF-2026-08-23-evening.md` §2 that this
   did not touch: research runs out at year 130, eras lag the tree, outlaw raid
   cadence, `roofEnough` was guessed.

## The pattern worth keeping

Three of the four commits above are the same move: **derive the look from fields
the content already has, add a small declared block only for what cannot be
derived, and make the generator refuse an entry that omits it.** A fallback that
never announces itself is how content ships broken — `Tools/generate.py`'s
`check` has a fourth gate now for exactly that.
