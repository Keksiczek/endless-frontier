## 1. What these two days changed

**The canvas grew a third dimension and then had to be argued down.** A
building is held above its own footprint and the gap is the wall face (§26);
the plot, the shadow and every hit test keep the map point. Two things went
wrong on the way and both were mine: the height was counted **twice**, so a
warehouse hung over the town with its goods inside it (§27.4), and the
attachment placement refused anything that did not fit whole inside a plot,
which for an ordinary house is every side position — so 54 names in

**A raid is now a raid you can answer.** The storyteller's raid resolved itself
in one step and left a record, so the fight was over before it was announced —
Keks was not missing the live surface, there was nothing to miss. It opens a
`Siege` like every other raid now (§28.1). The field also stopped clearing the
instant the fighting stopped: that linger was measured on a clock a raid pauses
(§29.1, rule 103's cure a third time).

**Four off the plan** (§30): the crafting gate is a ladder derived from chain
